Three rules the crew lives by

  1. 1

    Plan before reading

    The Foreman turns your request into work orders before anyone opens the inbox. A sneaky email can't edit a plan that was finished before it was read.

  2. 2

    One signed token per robot

    ArmorIQ signs each worker's slice. Support can refund and reply. Finance can pay. Publisher can post. Nobody can do everything.

  3. 3

    Every action passes the gate

    Each tool call goes through ArmorIQ's proxy: allowed, blocked, or held until a human says yes.
